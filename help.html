Frequently asked questions
How do I send money to another SwiftTrust customer?
From your dashboard, tap To SwiftTrust. Enter the recipient's 10-digit account number and the amount. You'll confirm the transfer with your 4-digit transaction PIN, then with a transfer code issued by SwiftTrust. Transfers are processed instantly.
What is a transfer code and where do I get one?
A transfer code is a unique authorization code issued by SwiftTrust for every transfer. It confirms that the transfer has been reviewed and approved. You can request a code by contacting customer service, and it will be delivered to you directly. Each code is single-use and expires after a set time.
How long do transfers take to arrive?
Transfers between SwiftTrust accounts are instant. Transfers to external banks typically settle the same business day. You'll see the transfer in your transaction history as soon as it's submitted.
Is there a fee for transfers?
SwiftTrust charges a flat 3% transfer fee on all outgoing transfers, whether to another SwiftTrust customer or an external bank. There are no monthly account fees, no minimum balance requirements, and no fees for incoming transfers.
What is High-Yield Savings?
High-Yield Savings is a savings account that lets you grow your balance at a competitive rate while keeping your funds separate from everyday spending. You can move money between Checking and High-Yield Savings any time using the Move money feature.
What is a Save lock?
A Save lock lets you commit funds for a fixed period — from 7 days up to 1 year — to earn 4.5% APY. Funds are held in the lock until the maturity date. When the lock matures, you can release the funds back to your checking account with a single tap.
How do I buy stocks?
Open the Stocks tab, choose a company from the Market, and tap Buy. Enter how many shares you want, review the order, and confirm with your transaction PIN. Stock purchases and sales settle instantly from your checking account.
How do I change my transaction PIN?
Go to Profile → Change transaction PIN. Enter your current PIN, then your new 4-digit PIN twice. If you've forgotten your PIN, contact customer service — we'll verify your identity and help you reset it.
How do I change my password?
Go to Profile → Change password. For security, you'll need to enter your current password first. Passwords must be at least 8 characters and should combine letters, numbers, and symbols.
What should I do if I lose my phone?
Contact us immediately using the Report fraud or a lost card option above. We'll temporarily freeze your account and help you regain access from a new device. Never share your password or transaction PIN with anyone — SwiftTrust will never ask for them.
I see a transaction I don't recognize. What now?
Open your Transaction History to review the full record. If you still don't recognize a charge, tap Report fraud at the top of this page. We investigate every report and will contact you within 24 hours.
How do I close my account?
Transfer any remaining funds to an external account, then contact customer service to request closure. Accounts with a non-zero balance cannot be closed until the balance is withdrawn. Any active Save locks must mature or be released first.
Keeping your account safe
Never share your PIN or password SwiftTrust will never call, text, or email asking for your password, transaction PIN, or one-time codes.
Watch for phishing Always check that the URL reads swifttrust before signing in. We never link to login pages from unexpected emails or texts.
Use a strong, unique password Combine upper and lower case letters, numbers, and symbols. Don't reuse a password from another site.
Review your activity regularly Check your transaction history once a week. If something looks off, report it immediately.
Sign out on shared devices Always sign out of your account when using a device you don't own.
